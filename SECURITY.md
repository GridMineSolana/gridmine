# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **gridminesol@gmail.com** with a description, affected instruction(s), and steps or a test to reproduce.
Do not open a public issue or disclose the bug before it is fixed.

We will acknowledge reports within 72 hours and keep you updated until a fix is deployed.
Please give us reasonable time to fix the issue before any public disclosure. We will credit reporters who want it.

## Scope

In scope: the GRIDMINE on-chain program in this repository, deployed on Solana mainnet at
`FAv6eoP4y6wJz2qCTDdKwcLQ2mE93trMi8sZpYdrvpoQ`.

Out of scope: the website, off-chain services, third-party programs (Jupiter, MagicBlock VRF, SPL Token), and
issues requiring a compromised admin or worker key.
